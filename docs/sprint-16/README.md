# Sprint 16 — Virtual Games, Results and Stats

Sprint 16 adds three deterministic virtual sports and unifies their history
with completed sports fixtures and a native competition table. Virtual rounds
are authenticated simulations with no stake or payout.

## Scope

- Football, basketball and racing virtual schedules.
- Deterministic, idempotent round simulation.
- Finished-fixture and virtual-history results dashboard.
- Competition table with owner-specific favourite state.
- Native Virtual Games and Results & Stats screens on iOS and Android.
